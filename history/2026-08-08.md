## 1974-08-08

On August 8, 1974, Richard Nixon became the first U.S. President to resign from office. Facing impeachment by the House of Representatives and certain conviction by the Senate for his role in the Watergate scandal, Nixon addressed the nation from the Oval Office. His resignation, effective the following day, marked a dramatic end to a tumultuous presidency. The scandal involved a break-in at the Democratic National Committee headquarters and subsequent cover-up, deeply shaking public trust in government.
