# Alcatraz Federal Penitentiary Receives Its First Civilian Inmates

![Alcatraz Federal Penitentiary Receives Its First Civilian Inmates](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/lookaside/crawler/media/?media_id=1173236754663203)

## 1934-08-11

On August 11, 1934, Alcatraz Federal Penitentiary, located on an island in San Francisco Bay, officially began its operation as a high-security federal prison by receiving its first batch of civilian prisoners. The former military prison was repurposed by the U.S. Department of Justice to house the most dangerous and incorrigible criminals in the American penal system. Under the supervision of its first warden, James A. Johnston, and Attorney General Homer S. Cummings, Alcatraz was specifically designed to be "the end of the line" for inmates. Its formidable island location and strict regimen made it seemingly escape-proof, quickly earning it the nickname "The Rock." This event marked the beginning of Alcatraz's infamous era as a symbol of federal power against organized crime during the Great Depression.
