# Partial Nuclear Test Ban Treaty Signed

![Partial Nuclear Test Ban Treaty Signed](https://diplomacy.state.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/bigger-cloud-pic.jpg)

## 1963-08-05

On August 5, 1963, the United States, the United Kingdom, and the Soviet Union signed the Partial Nuclear Test Ban Treaty (PTBT) in Moscow. This landmark agreement prohibited nuclear weapons tests in the atmosphere, outer space, and under water, though it permitted underground testing. The treaty was a direct response to growing public concern over radioactive fallout and a step towards de-escalating the Cold War's nuclear arms race, particularly after the Cuban Missile Crisis. It was eventually ratified by over 100 countries, representing a crucial moment in international arms control efforts and setting a precedent for future disarmament negotiations.
