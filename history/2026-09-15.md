# Enactment of the Nuremberg Laws

![Enactment of the Nuremberg Laws](https://www.archives.gov/files/publications/prologue/2010/winter/images/nuremberg-court-l.jpg)

## 1935-09-15

On September 15, 1935, Nazi Germany enacted the Nuremberg Laws at an annual Nazi Party rally held in Nuremberg. These antisemitic and racist statutes stripped Jews of their German citizenship and prohibited marriages and extramarital relations between Jews and Germans. The laws were a foundational step in the systematic persecution of Jews, clearly defining who was considered Jewish based on ancestry rather than religious practice. They laid the groundwork for further discriminatory legislation, marking a critical turning point towards the atrocities of the Holocaust. This event legally sanctioned state-sponsored discrimination, fundamentally altering the lives of Jewish people in Germany.
