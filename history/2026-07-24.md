# Apollo 11 Crew Returns to Earth

![Apollo 11 Crew Returns to Earth](https://www.fai.org/sites/default/files/styles/article_detail_xxlarge/public/apollo_11_recovery_0.jpg?itok=kSBIRcGI)

## 1969-07-24

After successfully completing humanity's first moon landing, the Apollo 11 command module 'Columbia' splashed down safely in the Pacific Ocean on July 24, 1969. Astronauts Neil Armstrong, Buzz Aldrin, and Michael Collins were recovered by the USS Hornet, approximately 900 miles southwest of Hawaii. The successful return marked the culmination of President John F. Kennedy's 1961 challenge to land a man on the Moon and return him safely to Earth before the end of the decade. The crew immediately entered quarantine due to unknown potential extraterrestrial microbes, but were later cleared and celebrated as global heroes. This momentous achievement solidified the United States' lead in the Space Race and captivated billions worldwide.
