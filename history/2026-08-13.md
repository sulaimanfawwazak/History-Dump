# Construction of the Berlin Wall Begins

![Construction of the Berlin Wall Begins](https://cdn.britannica.com/65/180265-138-827C0499/Overview-building-Berlin-Wall-1961.jpg)

## 1961-08-13

On August 13, 1961, the German Democratic Republic (GDR) began sealing off East Berlin from West Berlin by erecting barbed wire fences and barriers, initiating the construction of the Berlin Wall. This dramatic move was aimed at preventing East Germans from defecting to the capitalist West, which had led to a significant loss of skilled labor and economic stability for the GDR. The wall, a stark physical manifestation of the Iron Curtain, physically divided families and communities, transforming Berlin into a symbol of the Cold War's ideological divide. Over time, the initial barriers were replaced by a heavily fortified concrete wall, standing as a visible testament to the global geopolitical tensions until its fall in 1989.
