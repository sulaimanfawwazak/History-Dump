# New York City Subway System Opens

![New York City Subway System Opens](https://www.goldmansachs.com/images/migrated/our-firm/history/moments/150th-multimedia/1904-subway/1904-subway_800x450.jpg)

## 1904-10-27

On October 27, 1904, the Interborough Rapid Transit (IRT) company inaugurated the first line of the New York City Subway. The initial line stretched 9.1 miles, running from City Hall in Lower Manhattan to 145th Street and Broadway in Harlem. Mayor George B. McClellan Jr. drove the first train, marking a monumental shift in urban transportation for the bustling city. This event dramatically transformed daily life for millions of New Yorkers, offering a faster and more efficient way to navigate the metropolis. The subway system would eventually grow to become one of the largest and most complex public transit networks globally.
