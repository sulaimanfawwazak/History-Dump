# Neptune Discovered

![Neptune Discovered](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/pia01492-main.jpg)

## 1846-09-23

On September 23, 1846, the planet Neptune was officially discovered by German astronomer Johann Gottfried Galle at the Berlin Observatory, based on calculations by French mathematician Urbain Le Verrier. Le Verrier had mathematically predicted the existence and location of a new planet to account for irregularities in Uranus's orbit, a significant feat of celestial mechanics. Galle, assisted by Heinrich Louis d'Arrest, found Neptune less than a degree from Le Verrier's predicted position. This discovery marked a monumental triumph for Newtonian gravitational theory and mathematical astronomy, demonstrating the power of prediction in scientific inquiry.
