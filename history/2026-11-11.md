# Armistice Day Ends World War I

## 1918-11-11

On November 11, 1918, at 11:00 AM Paris time, an armistice signed in a railway carriage in the Compiègne Forest, France, officially ended hostilities on the Western Front of World War I. The agreement was signed by Allied commanders, primarily Marshal Ferdinand Foch, and German representatives, bringing to a close more than four years of brutal conflict. This momentous event marked the unconditional surrender of Germany and signified the complete defeat of the Central Powers. The armistice led to the eventual Treaty of Versailles and is commemorated annually as Armistice Day, Remembrance Day, or Veterans Day in many countries.
