# Nevado Del Ruiz Eruption Triggers Armero Disaster

## 1985-11-13

On November 13, 1985, the Nevado del Ruiz volcano in Colombia erupted, triggering massive lahars (volcanic mudslides). These deadly flows descended the volcano's slopes and tragically engulfed the town of Armero, located approximately 50 kilometers away. Despite prior warnings from geologists, local authorities failed to implement timely evacuation plans for the vulnerable population. The disaster claimed an estimated 25,000 lives, making it the second-deadliest volcanic event of the 20th century. This catastrophic event highlighted critical issues in disaster preparedness and risk communication worldwide.
