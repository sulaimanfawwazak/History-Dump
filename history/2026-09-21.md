## 1937-09-21

On September 21, 1937, J.R.R. Tolkien's classic fantasy novel, *The Hobbit, or There and Back Again*, was first published in the United Kingdom by George Allen & Unwin. This seminal work introduced readers to the world of Middle-earth, following the reluctant hobbit Bilbo Baggins on an unexpected journey with a company of dwarves and the wizard Gandalf to reclaim treasure from the dragon Smaug. Its immediate success led to calls for a sequel, prompting Tolkien to begin writing *The Lord of the Rings*. *The Hobbit* quickly gained critical acclaim and has since become one of the best-selling books of all time, establishing many foundational elements of modern high fantasy literature.
