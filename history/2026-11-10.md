# Sinking of the SS Edmund Fitzgerald

![Sinking of the SS Edmund Fitzgerald](https://shipwreckmuseum.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/edmund-fitzgerald.jpg)

## 1975-11-10

On November 10, 1975, the SS Edmund Fitzgerald, an American Great Lakes bulk carrier, tragically sank during a severe storm on Lake Superior. The vessel was transporting taconite pellets from Superior, Wisconsin, to a steel mill near Detroit, Michigan. All 29 crew members aboard, led by Captain Ernest M. McSorley, perished in the disaster, and no bodies were ever recovered. The exact cause of the sinking remains officially undetermined, despite extensive investigations and several theories, including structural failure, rogue waves, or cargo shift. This event became one of the most famous and mysterious maritime disasters in Great Lakes history, notably immortalized by Gordon Lightfoot's hit song, "The Wreck of the Edmund Fitzgerald."
