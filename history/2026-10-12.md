## 1492-10-12

On October 12, 1492, Genoese explorer Christopher Columbus, sailing under the Spanish flag, made landfall in the Americas, specifically on an island in the Bahamas which he named San Salvador. This momentous event marked the beginning of European colonization of the Americas, profoundly altering the course of world history and inaugurating an era known as the Columbian Exchange. Columbus believed he had reached the East Indies, a misconception that would persist for some time. His arrival initiated centuries of exploration, trade, and ultimately, significant cultural and demographic shifts across both continents, connecting two previously isolated worlds. This pivotal voyage opened new trade routes and led to widespread exchanges of goods, ideas, and populations.
