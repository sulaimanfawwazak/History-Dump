# Thomas Edison Invents Practical Incandescent Light Bulb

![Thomas Edison Invents Practical Incandescent Light Bulb](https://fi.edu/sites/default/files/styles/x_wide/public/2017-05/history-bulb1.jpg?itok=tutnJsae)

## 1879-10-21

On October 21, 1879, American inventor Thomas Edison successfully tested a durable, practical incandescent light bulb in his laboratory at Menlo Park, New Jersey. This revolutionary invention used a carbonized cotton thread filament and burned for over 13 hours, proving that electric lighting could be sustained and commercially viable. Edison's achievement marked a pivotal moment in technological history, paving the way for the widespread adoption of electricity and transforming daily life, industry, and urban landscapes globally. His subsequent work on a complete electrical distribution system further cemented the light bulb's place as a cornerstone of modern civilization.
