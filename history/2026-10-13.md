# Arrest Of The Knights Templar

## 1307-10-13

On Friday, October 13, 1307, agents of King Philip IV of France carried out a mass arrest of the Knights Templar across France. Accused of heresy, idolatry, sodomy, and various other charges, hundreds of Templars were simultaneously rounded up, including their Grand Master, Jacques de Molay. The arrests were orchestrated by King Philip IV, who was deeply indebted to the wealthy military order and sought to seize their considerable assets and eliminate their influence. This dramatic event marked the beginning of a brutal persecution that ultimately led to the dissolution of the Order of the Temple by Pope Clement V a few years later.
