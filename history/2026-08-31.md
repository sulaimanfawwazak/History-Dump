# Malaysia Achieves Independence

![Malaysia Achieves Independence](https://theposterific.files.wordpress.com/2014/08/merdeka1.jpg)

## 1957-08-31

On August 31, 1957, the Federation of Malaya, now known as Malaysia, achieved full independence from British colonial rule. This pivotal moment culminated years of negotiations and the tireless efforts of nationalist leaders, most notably Tunku Abdul Rahman, who subsequently became the nation's first Prime Minister. The historic declaration was made at Merdeka Stadium in Kuala Lumpur, before a jubilant crowd, officially ending more than a century of British administration. This event marked the birth of a new sovereign nation, laying the groundwork for its subsequent formation as Malaysia in 1963 with the inclusion of Singapore (until 1965), Sabah, and Sarawak, and its growth into a prominent Southeast Asian economy.
