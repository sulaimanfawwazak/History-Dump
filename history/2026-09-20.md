# Italian Troops Enter Rome, Completing Unification

## 1870-09-20

On September 20, 1870, Italian Bersaglieri troops breached the Aurelian Walls at Porta Pia and entered Rome, marking the final act of the Risorgimento, the process of Italian unification. This invasion effectively ended the temporal power of Pope Pius IX, who had resisted the unification of the Papal States with the new Kingdom of Italy. Rome was subsequently annexed to Italy, becoming its capital the following year. This event, known as the Capture of Rome, completed the long-sought political unity of the Italian peninsula under a single monarchy, bringing an end to the Papal States' millennial rule.
