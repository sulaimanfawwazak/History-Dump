# Maastricht Treaty Establishes The European Union

## 1993-11-01

On November 1, 1993, the Treaty on European Union, commonly known as the Maastricht Treaty, officially came into force. Signed in Maastricht, Netherlands, on February 7, 1992, by the twelve member states of the European Economic Community (EEC), this landmark agreement established the European Union (EU). It laid the groundwork for a common foreign and security policy, closer cooperation on justice and home affairs, and most notably, the creation of an economic and monetary union. This union ultimately led to the introduction of the euro currency. The treaty marked a significant step towards deeper political and economic integration among European nations, transforming the EEC into a more comprehensive political and economic entity.
