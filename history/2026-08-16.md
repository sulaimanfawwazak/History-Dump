## 1977-08-16

On August 16, 1977, the legendary 'King of Rock and Roll,' Elvis Presley, died at his Graceland mansion in Memphis, Tennessee, at the age of 42. His death was officially attributed to cardiac arrest, though years of prescription drug abuse were a significant contributing factor to his declining health. The sudden passing of one of the most iconic figures in music history sent shockwaves across the globe, leading to an immense outpouring of grief from millions of fans. Thousands gathered outside Graceland, turning his home into a pilgrimage site, and his funeral was a monumental event. His death cemented his status as a cultural icon, and he remains one of the best-selling and most influential musical artists of all time.
