# Albert Einstein Publishes His Theory of General Relativity

![Albert Einstein Publishes His Theory of General Relativity](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/lookaside/crawler/media/?media_id=1206276187812647)

## 1915-11-25

On November 25, 1915, Albert Einstein submitted his final paper on the theory of general relativity to the Prussian Academy of Sciences. This groundbreaking theory revolutionized physics by presenting a new understanding of gravity, explaining it as a curvature of spacetime caused by mass and energy, rather than a force. It expanded upon his earlier special relativity and provided a unified description of gravity as a geometric property of spacetime. The theory had profound implications, predicting phenomena like black holes, gravitational lensing, and the expansion of the universe. Its publication marked a pivotal moment in scientific history, fundamentally altering our perception of the cosmos.
