## 1978-07-25

Louise Joy Brown, the first human born through in vitro fertilization (IVF), was born in Oldham, England. Her birth marked a monumental breakthrough in reproductive medicine, offering hope to millions of infertile couples worldwide. The procedure was pioneered by British physiologist Robert Edwards and gynecologist Patrick Steptoe, who faced significant ethical debate and skepticism throughout their research. Louise's birth proved the viability and safety of IVF, fundamentally changing the landscape of fertility treatment and reproductive science. Edwards later received the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine in 2010 for his work.
