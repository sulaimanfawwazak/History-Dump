## 1571-10-07

On October 7, 1571, the Battle of Lepanto took place in the Gulf of Patras, off the western coast of Greece. This monumental naval engagement pitted the Holy League, a coalition of Catholic maritime states (primarily Spain, Venice, and the Papal States), against the Ottoman Empire. Led by Don John of Austria, the Holy League secured a decisive victory, inflicting heavy losses on the Ottoman fleet. The battle effectively halted Ottoman expansion into the western Mediterranean and was a crucial moment in the centuries-long struggle between Christian and Islamic powers. It is widely considered one of the most important naval battles in history.
