# Large Hadron Collider Activated at CERN

![Large Hadron Collider Activated at CERN](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/lookaside/crawler/media/?media_id=726655019908082)

## 2008-09-10

On September 10, 2008, the Large Hadron Collider (LHC), the world's largest and most powerful particle accelerator, was officially switched on at CERN near Geneva, Switzerland. The first beam of protons was successfully circulated around its 27-kilometer (17-mile) underground ring, marking a monumental step in experimental particle physics. Built in collaboration with over 10,000 scientists and engineers from over 100 countries, the LHC was designed to test various predictions of particle physics, including the existence of the Higgs boson. Its activation heralded a new era in scientific discovery, eventually leading to the confirmation of the Higgs boson in 2012 and profound insights into the fundamental nature of the universe.
