# United Kingdom Declares War On Germany

![United Kingdom Declares War On Germany](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2d/Britain_declares_war--Daily_Mail_Aug_5%2C_1914.jpg)

## 1914-08-04

On August 4, 1914, the United Kingdom officially declared war on Germany, marking Britain's entry into World War I. This pivotal decision came after Germany violated Belgium's neutrality by invading the country as part of the Schlieffen Plan, aimed at rapidly defeating France. The British ultimatum demanding German withdrawal from Belgium expired at 11 PM GMT, leading to the declaration. Britain's entry brought the vast resources of its global empire into the conflict, significantly expanding the scope and scale of what had previously been a continental European war. This event fundamentally reshaped the geopolitical landscape of the 20th century.
