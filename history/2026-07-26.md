# Egypt Nationalizes Suez Canal

![Egypt Nationalizes Suez Canal](http://www.egypttoday.com/siteimages/Larg/202207270236463646.jpg)

## 1956-07-26

On July 26, 1956, Egyptian President Gamal Abdel Nasser announced the nationalization of the Suez Canal Company. This bold move seized control of the vital waterway from its French and British owners, effectively transferring its operation and revenues to Egypt. Nasser justified the nationalization by stating that the income would finance the construction of the Aswan High Dam, after the United States and Britain withdrew their offers to fund the project. The decision triggered the Suez Crisis later that year, involving military intervention by Israel, Britain, and France. This event solidified Nasser's pan-Arab leadership and marked a significant moment in post-colonial history.
