## 1973-10-20

On October 20, 1973, in an event dubbed the "Saturday Night Massacre," U.S. President Richard Nixon ordered Attorney General Elliot Richardson to fire Watergate special prosecutor Archibald Cox. Cox had subpoenaed secret White House tapes, which Nixon steadfastly refused to release. Rather than comply with the order, Richardson resigned in protest, as did Deputy Attorney General William Ruckelshaus, who also refused. Ultimately, Solicitor General Robert Bork carried out the order, dismissing Cox. This dramatic series of events ignited a constitutional crisis and further fueled public outrage, significantly intensifying calls for Nixon's impeachment.
