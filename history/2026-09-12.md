## 1940-09-12

On September 12, 1940, a group of four teenagers stumbled upon the famous Lascaux Cave in Montignac, France. Marcel Ravidat, Jacques Marsal, Georges Agnel, and Simon Coencas were looking for their dog when they found a hidden entrance. Inside, they discovered thousands of stunning prehistoric cave paintings, dating back approximately 17,000 years to the Paleolithic era. These vivid depictions of animals, human figures, and abstract signs quickly became one of the most significant archaeological finds of the 20th century. The discovery provided invaluable insights into the art and culture of early modern humans.
