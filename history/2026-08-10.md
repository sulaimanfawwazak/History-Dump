# Missouri Admitted As 24th U.S. State

![Missouri Admitted As 24th U.S. State](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/lookaside/crawler/media/?media_id=3943726572405878)

## 1821-08-10

On August 10, 1821, Missouri officially became the 24th state to be admitted to the United States. This admission followed the intense national debate over slavery, which was temporarily resolved by the Missouri Compromise of 1820. The compromise allowed Missouri to enter as a slave state while Maine was admitted as a free state, maintaining the balance of power between free and slave states in the Senate. This pivotal moment highlighted the growing sectional tensions that would ultimately lead to the American Civil War, despite the temporary legislative solution.
