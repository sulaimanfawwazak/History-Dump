## 1776-09-09

On September 9, 1776, the Second Continental Congress, meeting in Philadelphia, officially changed the name of the confederation of states from "United Colonies" to "United States of America." This landmark decision formalized the identity of the nascent nation, which had declared its independence from Great Britain just over two months prior. The new designation reflected a move towards a unified sovereign entity rather than a collection of dependent territories. This act was a significant step in the American Revolution, solidifying the national character and purpose as the newly independent states sought to forge a common destiny. It marked a crucial moment in the foundation of the American republic.
