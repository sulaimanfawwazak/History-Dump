# British General John Burgoyne Surrenders At Saratoga

![British General John Burgoyne Surrenders At Saratoga](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/71/Surrender_of_General_Burgoyne.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=index&utm_content=original)

## 1777-10-17

On October 17, 1777, British General John Burgoyne surrendered his entire army to American forces under General Horatio Gates at Saratoga, New York, during the American Revolutionary War. This pivotal victory marked a significant turning point in the war, boosting American morale and convincing France to formally ally with the United States. The Battle of Saratoga is considered one of the most decisive battles in military history. Burgoyne's plan to divide the New England colonies from the others ultimately failed, leading to a crucial diplomatic and military advantage for the American cause.
