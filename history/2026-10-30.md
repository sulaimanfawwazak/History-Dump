# Orson Welles's War Of The Worlds Broadcast

![Orson Welles's War Of The Worlds Broadcast](https://www.neh.gov/sites/default/files/2018-10/Orson_Welles-01-square.jpg)

## 1938-10-30

On October 30, 1938, Orson Welles and the Mercury Theatre on the Air broadcast a radio adaptation of H.G. Wells's classic novel, *The War of the Worlds*. Presented as a series of realistic breaking news bulletins and eyewitness accounts, the dramatization described an alien invasion from Mars. Despite opening disclaimers, many listeners tuned in late and mistook the fictional drama for actual news reports, leading to widespread panic and confusion across the United States. This event famously highlighted the immense power of radio as a medium and sparked significant public discussion about media literacy and mass hysteria.
