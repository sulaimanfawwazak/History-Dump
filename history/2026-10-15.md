# Mata Hari Executed for Espionage

![Mata Hari Executed for Espionage](https://history.blog.gov.uk/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2017/10/large_000000-e1507882908953.jpg)

## 1917-10-15

On October 15, 1917, Margaretha Geertruida MacLeod, better known as Mata Hari, was executed by firing squad outside Paris, France. The Dutch exotic dancer and courtesan was convicted by a French military court of being a German spy during World War I. Despite maintaining her innocence, alleging she was a double agent for the French, she was found guilty of espionage and causing the deaths of thousands of soldiers. Her trial was highly publicized, and her story has since become a legendary tale of glamour, intrigue, and betrayal, though historical debate continues regarding the extent of her actual guilt.
