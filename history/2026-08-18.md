# Nineteenth Amendment Ratified Granting Women's Suffrage

![Nineteenth Amendment Ratified Granting Women's Suffrage](https://www.thenation.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/19th_amendment_loc_img.jpg)

## 1920-08-18

On August 18, 1920, the Nineteenth Amendment to the United States Constitution was officially ratified. This landmark amendment declared that the right of citizens of the United States to vote shall not be denied or abridged by the United States or by any State on account of sex. Its ratification was the culmination of a decades-long struggle by the women's suffrage movement, involving countless activists and protests across the nation. Tennessee became the 36th state to ratify the amendment, providing the crucial three-fourths majority needed for its adoption. The passage of the Nineteenth Amendment marked a monumental victory for civil rights and significantly expanded democratic participation in the U.S.
