# Eruption of Mount Vesuvius Buries Pompeii

![Eruption of Mount Vesuvius Buries Pompeii](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/lookaside/crawler/media/?media_id=811117235292162)

## 0079-08-24

On August 24, 79 AD, Mount Vesuvius erupted catastrophically, unleashing a deadly cloud of stones, ash, and volcanic gases over the nearby Roman cities of Pompeii and Herculaneum. The eruption, which lasted for two days, buried Pompeii under meters of ash and pumice, preserving an eerie snapshot of daily life as its inhabitants attempted to flee or were engulfed. Herculaneum, situated closer to the volcano, was overwhelmed by pyroclastic flows, its residents instantly vaporized. The cities remained lost for over 1,500 years, offering unparalleled insights into Roman civilization upon their rediscovery.
