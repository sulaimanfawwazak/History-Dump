# Mexico's War of Independence Begins

![Mexico's War of Independence Begins](https://www.tshaonline.org/images/handbook/entries/MM/mexican-independence.jpg)

## 1810-09-16

On September 16, 1810, Father Miguel Hidalgo y Costilla issued the "Grito de Dolores" (Cry of Dolores) from his church in the town of Dolores, Guanajuato. This impassioned call to arms urged Mexicans to revolt against Spanish rule, marking the beginning of Mexico's War of Independence. Thousands of peasants and indigenous people rallied behind Hidalgo, initiating a long and bloody struggle for sovereignty against colonial power. Although Hidalgo himself was captured and executed less than a year later, his cry became the symbolic start of the fight that eventually led to Mexico's independence in 1821, and the date is celebrated annually as Mexico's Independence Day.
