# Hurricane Katrina Makes Landfall

![Hurricane Katrina Makes Landfall](https://www.weather.gov/images/mob/events/katrina/Hurr_Katrina_29Aug_Satellite2.png)

## 2005-08-29

Hurricane Katrina, one of the deadliest and costliest hurricanes in U.S. history, made landfall in Louisiana on August 29, 2005, primarily devastating the city of New Orleans and the Gulf Coast. The Category 3 storm caused catastrophic damage due to its powerful winds and an unprecedented storm surge that led to widespread levee failures, flooding 80% of New Orleans. The disaster resulted in over 1,800 fatalities and an estimated $125 billion in damages, sparking a massive but often criticized federal response. Its aftermath highlighted significant issues in disaster preparedness, urban infrastructure, and socio-economic inequalities.
