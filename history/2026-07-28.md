# Austria-Hungary Declares War on Serbia, Starting World War I

![Austria-Hungary Declares War on Serbia, Starting World War I](https://www.thenation.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/sarajevo_riots_1914_cc_img.jpg)

## 1914-07-28

On July 28, 1914, Austria-Hungary declared war on Serbia, officially commencing World War I. This declaration followed a month-long diplomatic crisis, known as the July Crisis, triggered by the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria by a Serbian nationalist on June 28. A series of escalating ultimatums and rejections ultimately led to the conflict. Due to a complex web of military alliances, the war rapidly expanded to involve the major European powers and eventually engulfed much of the world, resulting in unprecedented global devastation.
