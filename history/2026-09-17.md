# The United States Constitution Is Signed

![The United States Constitution Is Signed](https://constitutioncenter.org/images/uploads/blog/640px-Scene_at_the_Signing_of_the_Constitution_of_the_United_States.jpg)

## 1787-09-17

On September 17, 1787, at Independence Hall in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, a majority of the delegates to the Constitutional Convention affixed their signatures to the United States Constitution. This landmark document established the framework for the federal government and defined the rights and responsibilities of citizens, replacing the weaker Articles of Confederation. The signing represented a pivotal moment in American history, as it laid the groundwork for a stronger, more unified national government. Although not all delegates present signed the final draft, their approval initiated the critical process of ratification by the states, which led to its ultimate adoption and implementation.
