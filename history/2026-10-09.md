# Che Guevara Captured

![Che Guevara Captured](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/styles/wide/public/thumbnails/image/che_guevara_after_his_execution.jpg?itok=H6CpvY1b)

## 1967-10-09

Revolutionary icon Ernesto 'Che' Guevara was captured on October 9, 1967, in Bolivia, following a failed guerrilla insurgency aimed at sparking a continent-wide revolution. After months of being tracked by Bolivian Special Forces, who were aided by U.S. CIA advisors, Guevara's small rebel column was cornered in the Yuro Ravine near La Higuera. Wounded and surrounded, he was taken prisoner and transported to a schoolhouse in La Higuera. This capture effectively ended his armed struggle in South America. He was controversially executed the very next day, solidifying his image as a martyr for many revolutionary causes worldwide.
