# Martin Luther Posts His 95 Theses, Igniting the Protestant Reformation

![Martin Luther Posts His 95 Theses, Igniting the Protestant Reformation](https://americanheritage.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/02/Luther-95-Theses-Cropped-2.jpg)

## 1517-10-31

On October 31, 1517, German monk and theologian Martin Luther famously posted his Ninety-five Theses on the door of All Saints' Church in Wittenberg, Saxony. This academic challenge was a critique of various practices of the Catholic Church, particularly the selling of indulgences, which promised the remission of temporal punishment for sins. Luther's action, intended as an invitation to debate, quickly spread due to the recent invention of the printing press, reaching a wide audience across Europe. It marked a pivotal moment that is widely considered the catalyst for the Protestant Reformation, fundamentally altering the religious and political landscape of the Western world. The event challenged papal authority and led to the formation of new Christian denominations.
