# Warsaw Pact Invasion of Czechoslovakia

![Warsaw Pact Invasion of Czechoslovakia](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/6c/Invasion_of_Czechoslovakia_collage.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=index&utm_content=original)

## 1968-08-21

On August 21, 1968, the Soviet Union and other Warsaw Pact members launched a surprise invasion of Czechoslovakia. This military action, involving hundreds of thousands of troops and thousands of tanks, aimed to crush the 'Prague Spring' reforms initiated by Alexander Dubček's government, which sought to liberalize the communist regime. The invasion met with non-violent resistance from the Czechoslovakian people but effectively ended the reforms. It solidified Soviet control over the Eastern Bloc and was a significant event in the Cold War, demonstrating the Brezhnev Doctrine's enforcement.
