# Atomic Bomb Dropped on Nagasaki

![Atomic Bomb Dropped on Nagasaki](https://media.newyorker.com/photos/59096f87ebe912338a376df7/master/w_2560%2Cc_limit/Wellerstein-Nagasaki.jpg)

## 1945-08-09

On August 9, 1945, three days after the bombing of Hiroshima, the United States dropped a second atomic bomb, codenamed "Fat Man," on the Japanese city of Nagasaki. The B-29 bomber Bockscar, piloted by Major Charles Sweeney, released the plutonium-based bomb over the city at 11:02 AM local time. The initial target was Kokura, but heavy cloud cover forced a diversion to Nagasaki. The explosion instantly killed an estimated 40,000 people and devastated a significant portion of the city. This catastrophic event, alongside the bombing of Hiroshima, contributed to Japan's unconditional surrender six days later, effectively ending World War II.
