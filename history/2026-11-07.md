# The October Revolution Begins

## 1917-11-07

On November 7, 1917 (October 25th by the Julian calendar then used in Russia), the Bolshevik Party, led by Vladimir Lenin, launched an armed insurrection in Petrograd (now St. Petersburg). This coup, often referred to as the October Revolution, overthrew the Provisional Government that had been established after the abdication of Tsar Nicholas II earlier that year. Key targets like the Winter Palace, post offices, and railway stations were seized by Bolshevik Red Guards and revolutionary soldiers and sailors. This event marked the beginning of Soviet rule and profoundly reshaped the political landscape of Russia, leading to the establishment of the Soviet Union and decades of communist governance. The revolution's impact resonated globally, influencing socialist movements and geopolitical dynamics throughout the 20th century.
