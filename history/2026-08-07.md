# Guadalcanal Campaign Begins

![Guadalcanal Campaign Begins](https://cdn.britannica.com/60/11360-050-B2D9DDAD/marines-Guadalcanal-land-offensive-theater-World-War-August-1942.jpg)

## 1942-08-07

On August 7, 1942, during World War II, the United States launched its first major offensive operation against the Empire of Japan, landing Marines on Guadalcanal and nearby islands in the Solomon Islands. This marked the beginning of the grueling six-month Guadalcanal Campaign, a critical turning point in the Pacific Theater. The primary objective was to capture the airfield being constructed by the Japanese, which would then be used to protect Allied supply lines between the U.S., Australia, and New Zealand. The brutal fighting, often hand-to-hand in dense jungle and across the sea, resulted in heavy casualties for both sides but ultimately secured a strategic victory for the Allies.
