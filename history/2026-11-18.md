# Steamboat Willie Premieres

![Steamboat Willie Premieres](https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/americanexperience/media/gallery_images/18_2511M.jpg)

## 1928-11-18

On November 18, 1928, Walt Disney's animated short film 'Steamboat Willie' premiered at the Colony Theater in New York City. This landmark cartoon was significant for being one of the first animated films with synchronized sound, effectively launching the 'Golden Age of Animation.' More importantly, it marked the official debut of Mickey Mouse, who would go on to become one of the most recognizable characters in the world and a global cultural icon. The film's success propelled Walt Disney Productions into a new era of innovation and popularity.
