## 1922-11-26

On November 26, 1922, British archaeologist Howard Carter, accompanied by his patron Lord Carnarvon, made the historic first breach into the inner chambers of the tomb of Pharaoh Tutankhamun in Egypt's Valley of the Kings. Carter had discovered the tomb's outer steps earlier that month, and this day marked the moment they peered into the antechamber, revealing a vast collection of ancient treasures. His famous response to Carnarvon's question, 'Can you see anything?', was 'Yes, wonderful things!'. This discovery was unique as the tomb was largely intact, providing an unparalleled glimpse into ancient Egyptian funerary practices and royal wealth. The subsequent excavation and cataloging of thousands of artifacts captivated the world and significantly advanced Egyptology.
