## 1781-10-19

On October 19, 1781, British General Charles Cornwallis formally surrendered his 8,000-man army to a combined American and French force at Yorktown, Virginia. The allied forces were led by General George Washington and Comte de Rochambeau, with crucial naval support from French Admiral de Grasse blocking British escape by sea. This decisive victory marked the last major land battle of the American Revolutionary War, concluding a month-long siege. The surrender profoundly impacted British public opinion and effectively secured American independence, though the Treaty of Paris formally ended the war in 1783.
