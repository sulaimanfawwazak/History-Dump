# Germany Invades Poland, Beginning World War II

![Germany Invades Poland, Beginning World War II](https://www.nationalww2museum.org/sites/default/files/2023-10/Bundesarchiv_Bild_183-S55480%2C_Polen%2C_Parade_vor_Adolf_Hitler.jpg)

## 1939-09-01

On September 1, 1939, Nazi Germany launched a surprise invasion of Poland, officially marking the beginning of World War II. Adolf Hitler's forces employed the new 'Blitzkrieg' strategy, utilizing rapid mechanized units and air power to quickly overcome Polish defenses. This aggressive act prompted Great Britain and France to declare war on Germany two days later, fulfilling their mutual defense treaties with Poland. The invasion shattered the fragile peace in Europe that had been established after World War I, escalating tensions into a global conflict. This event initiated a devastating six-year war that reshaped the world's geopolitical landscape and resulted in tens of millions of deaths.
