# Alaska Formally Transferred to the United States

![Alaska Formally Transferred to the United States](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/lookaside/crawler/media/?media_id=418755482950574)

## 1867-10-18

The formal transfer of Alaska from Russia to the United States occurred on October 18, 1867, at Sitka, Alaska. This transaction, known as the Alaska Purchase, involved the United States acquiring the vast territory for $7.2 million, or approximately two cents per acre. U.S. General Lovell Rousseau and Russian Captain Aleksei Peshchurov oversaw the flag-raising ceremony, symbolizing the peaceful transition of sovereignty. The acquisition, spearheaded by U.S. Secretary of State William H. Seward, was initially mocked as "Seward's Folly" but proved to be a strategic move, rich in natural resources and significantly expanding U.S. territory.
