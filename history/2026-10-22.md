# President Kennedy Announces Cuban Missile Crisis Blockade

## 1962-10-22

On October 22, 1962, U.S. President John F. Kennedy addressed the nation in a televised speech, informing the American public and the world about the discovery of Soviet nuclear missile bases under construction in Cuba. He announced the imposition of a naval 'quarantine' around Cuba to prevent further Soviet arms shipments, demanding the removal of the offensive weapons already present. This dramatic declaration escalated the Cold War to its most dangerous point, bringing the United States and the Soviet Union to the brink of nuclear conflict. The world watched tensely as a thirteen-day standoff unfolded, ultimately resolved through intense diplomatic negotiations.
