# Christopher Columbus Sets Sail from Spain

![Christopher Columbus Sets Sail from Spain](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/B4bj1frv8Sg/maxresdefault.jpg)

## 1492-08-03

On August 3, 1492, explorer Christopher Columbus departed from Palos de la Frontera, Spain, with three ships: the Niña, the Pinta, and the Santa María. His expedition, funded by the Spanish monarchs Ferdinand II and Isabella I, aimed to find a westward sea route to Asia. This voyage, driven by the desire for new trade routes and the spread of Christianity, marked the beginning of a transformative era of European exploration and colonization of the Americas. While he did not reach Asia, his landing in the Caribbean later that year profoundly altered global history and geography.
