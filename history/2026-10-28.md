# Statue of Liberty Dedicated

![Statue of Liberty Dedicated](https://bowienewsonline.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/statueplaque.jpg)

## 1886-10-28

The Statue of Liberty, a monumental gift from the people of France to the United States, was formally dedicated on Bedloe's Island (now Liberty Island) in New York Harbor. President Grover Cleveland presided over the elaborate ceremony, which drew hundreds of thousands of spectators and a grand naval parade. Designed by French sculptor Frédéric Auguste Bartholdi and engineered by Gustave Eiffel, the statue quickly became an iconic symbol of freedom, democracy, and welcoming immigrants. This dedication solidified the enduring friendship between France and the United States, marking a significant milestone in both nations' histories.
