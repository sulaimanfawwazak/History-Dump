## 1981-08-01

On August 1, 1981, Music Television (MTV) made its debut in the United States, revolutionizing the music and television industries. The first music video ever played on the channel was "Video Killed the Radio Star" by The Buggles, setting the tone for a new era of visual music. MTV quickly became a dominant cultural force, shaping youth trends, launching careers of numerous artists, and influencing fashion and advertising. Its initial focus on music videos gradually expanded to include reality shows and other programming, but its foundational impact on popular culture remains significant.
