## 1990-08-02

On August 2, 1990, the Republic of Iraq, under the leadership of Saddam Hussein, launched a full-scale invasion of its smaller, oil-rich neighbor, Kuwait. Iraqi forces quickly overwhelmed Kuwaiti defenses, leading to the occupation and subsequent annexation of the country. This act of aggression was widely condemned internationally and immediately triggered a massive global crisis, culminating in the deployment of multinational forces to the region. The invasion ultimately set the stage for the Gulf War (Operation Desert Shield/Storm) just a few months later.
