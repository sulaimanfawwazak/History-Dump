# Formal Surrender of Japan Marks End of World War II

![Formal Surrender of Japan Marks End of World War II](https://media.defense.gov/2015/Sep/10/2001288999/1920/1080/0/150911-F-ZZ999-002.JPG)

## 1945-09-02

On September 2, 1945, World War II officially concluded with the formal surrender of the Empire of Japan aboard the USS Missouri in Tokyo Bay. Japanese Foreign Minister Mamoru Shigemitsu signed the Instrument of Surrender on behalf of Emperor Hirohito and the Japanese government, while General Yoshijiro Umezu signed for the Japanese Imperial General Headquarters. General Douglas MacArthur, the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, accepted the surrender for the Allied nations. This momentous event, celebrated as V-J Day (Victory over Japan Day), brought an end to over six years of global conflict and devastation. The ceremony marked the definitive close of the deadliest war in human history, ushering in a new era of international relations.
