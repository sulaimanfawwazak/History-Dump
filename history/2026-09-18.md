# United States Air Force Established

![United States Air Force Established](https://media.defense.gov/2009/Aug/25/2000495412/2000/2000/0/090825-F-1234O-011.JPG)

## 1947-09-18

On September 18, 1947, the United States Air Force was officially established as a separate branch of the U.S. armed forces. This momentous event, stemming from the National Security Act of 1947, recognized air power as a distinct and critical component of national defense. Previously, air operations had been under the Army as the U.S. Army Air Forces. The creation of the USAF marked a pivotal shift in military strategy and organization, acknowledging the increasing importance of air superiority in modern warfare. This foundational act reshaped America's military structure and its approach to national security in the post-World War II era.
