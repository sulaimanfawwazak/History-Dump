# Luna 2 Becomes First Man-Made Object to Reach the Moon

![Luna 2 Becomes First Man-Made Object to Reach the Moon](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/lookaside/crawler/media/?media_id=122201751464286052)

## 1959-09-14

On September 14, 1959, the Soviet Union's Luna 2 spacecraft successfully impacted the Moon, making it the first man-made object to reach the lunar surface. Launched just two days prior from Baikonur Cosmodrome, the probe carried a sphere containing Soviet pennants, which were designed to scatter on impact. This groundbreaking achievement provided crucial data on the Moon's environment, proving the absence of a significant magnetic field and radiation belts. It marked a significant victory for the Soviet Union in the early stages of the Space Race and demonstrated the capability to send objects beyond Earth's orbit, directly influencing subsequent lunar missions by both the USSR and the United States.
