# China Detonates Its First Atomic Bomb

![China Detonates Its First Atomic Bomb](https://thechinaproject.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/The-story-of-Chinas-first-atomic-bomb.jpg)

## 1964-10-16

On October 16, 1964, the People's Republic of China successfully detonated its first atomic bomb, codenamed "596," at the Lop Nur nuclear weapons test site in Xinjiang province. This historic event marked China's entry into the exclusive club of nuclear powers, making it the fifth nation to possess nuclear weapons. The test significantly altered the global geopolitical landscape, enhancing China's strategic standing and adding a new dimension to the Cold War dynamics. It was a major achievement for China's scientific and engineering capabilities, demonstrating its commitment to self-reliance in defense and projecting its influence on the world stage.
