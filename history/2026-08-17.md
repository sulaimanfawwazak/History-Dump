# Indonesia Declares Independence

![Indonesia Declares Independence](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f1/Indonesia_declaration_of_independence_17_August_1945.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=index&utm_content=original)

## 1945-08-17

On August 17, 1945, Sukarno, leader of the Indonesian National Party, and Mohammad Hatta formally proclaimed Indonesia's independence from Dutch colonial rule. This pivotal declaration took place just days after Japan's surrender in World War II, which created a power vacuum following the end of Japanese occupation. Although the Dutch attempted to re-establish control, a four-year armed and diplomatic struggle, known as the Indonesian National Revolution, ensued. The declaration marked the beginning of modern Indonesia as a sovereign nation, inspiring similar independence movements across Asia and Africa.
