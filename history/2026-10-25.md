# Battle Of Agincourt

## 1415-10-25

The Battle of Agincourt took place on October 25, 1415, near Azincourt in northern France, during the Hundred Years' War. It was a decisive English victory against a numerically superior French army. Led by King Henry V, the English longbowmen decimated the French cavalry and men-at-arms, despite being exhausted and outnumbered. This battle significantly boosted English morale and prestige, marking a high point in Henry V's campaign and demonstrating the effectiveness of massed archers against heavily armored knights. It remains one of England's most famous military victories.
