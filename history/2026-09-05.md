## 1774-09-05

On September 5, 1774, the First Continental Congress convened in Carpenter's Hall in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. Delegates from twelve of the thirteen British North American colonies (Georgia did not send representatives) gathered to discuss a unified response to the British Parliament's Intolerable Acts. This meeting marked a significant step toward American independence, as the colonies began to act collectively against perceived British oppression. The Congress issued a Declaration of Rights and Grievances and called for a boycott of British goods, laying the groundwork for future revolutionary action.
