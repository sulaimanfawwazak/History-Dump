## 1860-11-06

On November 6, 1860, Abraham Lincoln, the Republican candidate, was elected as the 16th President of the United States. His victory was largely due to the split in the Democratic Party, which ran two candidates, Stephen Douglas and John C. Breckinridge, along with John Bell of the Constitutional Union Party. Although Lincoln won with only about 40% of the popular vote, he secured a clear majority in the Electoral College. His election immediately triggered the secession crisis, leading several Southern states to declare their withdrawal from the Union before his inauguration. This pivotal event dramatically altered the course of American history, directly setting the stage for the American Civil War.
