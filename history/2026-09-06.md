# U.S. President William McKinley Is Shot

![U.S. President William McKinley Is Shot](https://cdn.britannica.com/59/181159-050-A4470A1E/Leon-Czolgosz-police-mug-shot-Pres-William-September-1901.jpg)

## 1901-09-06

On September 6, 1901, U.S. President William McKinley was shot twice by anarchist Leon Czolgosz during a public reception at the Pan-American Exposition in Buffalo, New York. McKinley had just delivered a speech at the exposition and was greeting the public when Czolgosz, with a pistol concealed by a handkerchief, fired the shots. The president initially seemed to be recovering from his wounds. However, his condition worsened due to infection, and he died eight days later, on September 14, making Theodore Roosevelt the new president.
