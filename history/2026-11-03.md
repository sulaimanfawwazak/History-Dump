## 1957-11-03

On November 3, 1957, the Soviet Union launched Sputnik 2, its second spacecraft, carrying the dog Laika. This mission marked a significant milestone as Laika became the first living creature to orbit Earth, providing crucial data on how a living organism would react to spaceflight. Although Laika tragically perished hours after launch due to overheating, her pioneering journey paved the way for human space travel. The launch intensified the Space Race with the United States and demonstrated the Soviet Union's advanced rocket capabilities. This event captured global attention and underlined the profound scientific and propaganda implications of early space exploration.
