# Galveston Hurricane of 1900 Strikes Texas

![Galveston Hurricane of 1900 Strikes Texas](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/73/1900_Galveston_hurricane_weather_map_%28high_quality%29_%2819877020%29.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=index&utm_content=original)

## 1900-09-08

On September 8, 1900, a devastating Category 4 hurricane made landfall on Galveston Island, Texas, becoming the deadliest natural disaster in United States history. The powerful storm generated a massive storm surge, estimated at 15 feet, which completely inundated the low-lying city. Due to a lack of adequate warning systems, thousands of residents were caught unprepared, leading to catastrophic loss of life. Estimates vary, but between 6,000 and 12,000 people perished, and the city was largely destroyed. This tragedy prompted major infrastructure changes, including the construction of a 17-foot high seawall and a grade-raising project for the city.
