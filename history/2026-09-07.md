## 1822-09-07

On September 7, 1822, Prince Dom Pedro, regent of Brazil, famously declared the country's independence from Portugal. This pivotal moment occurred by the Ipiranga River near São Paulo, when he reportedly uttered the cry "Independence or Death!" Dom Pedro had been urged to return to Portugal, where a new liberal government sought to reassert control over Brazil, a move unpopular with Brazilian elites. His declaration thwarted attempts by the Portuguese Cortes to revert Brazil to its colonial status. The event led to the establishment of the independent Empire of Brazil, with Dom Pedro soon crowned as its first emperor, Dom Pedro I.
