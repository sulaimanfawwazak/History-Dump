## 1911-10-10

On October 10, 1911, the Wuchang Uprising began in Wuchang, Hubei province, China. This event marked the formal start of the Xinhai Revolution, a nationwide rebellion that ultimately led to the collapse of the Qing Dynasty, China's last imperial dynasty. The uprising was sparked by an accidental bomb explosion and a subsequent police investigation, which revealed a revolutionary plot. Led by the New Army, disillusioned with the Qing government's perceived weakness and foreign dominance, the revolt quickly spread to other provinces. Within months, the Qing emperor abdicated, paving the way for the establishment of the Republic of China in 1912.
