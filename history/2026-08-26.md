# Nineteenth Amendment To The U.S. Constitution Certified

![Nineteenth Amendment To The U.S. Constitution Certified](https://www.archives.gov/files/milestone-documents/images/doc-063-big.jpg)

## 1920-08-26

On August 26, 1920, the 19th Amendment to the United States Constitution was officially certified by Secretary of State Bainbridge Colby. This pivotal amendment enshrined women's right to vote across the nation, culminating a struggle that spanned over seven decades. The certification followed intense campaigning by suffragists like Susan B. Anthony and Elizabeth Cady Stanton, who advocated for equal political participation. With its ratification, millions of American women gained full suffrage, fundamentally changing the electorate and marking a monumental achievement for the women's rights movement in the United States. This day is now celebrated as Women's Equality Day.
