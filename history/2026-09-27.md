# Mexico Achieves Independence from Spain

![Mexico Achieves Independence from Spain](https://www.tshaonline.org/images/handbook/entries/MM/mexican-independence.jpg)

## 1821-09-27

On September 27, 1821, Mexico formally achieved its independence from Spain, concluding an eleven-year war for sovereignty. This pivotal moment occurred when the Army of the Three Guarantees, led by Agustín de Iturbide, triumphantly marched into Mexico City. The declaration of independence followed the earlier signing of the Treaty of Córdoba, which recognized Mexico as an independent nation under the Plan of Iguala. This event marked the end of over 300 years of Spanish colonial rule and the establishment of the First Mexican Empire.
