# Yom Kippur War Begins

![Yom Kippur War Begins](https://cdn.britannica.com/86/226186-050-C66114DB/Israeli-armoured-column-Yom-Kippur-War-1973.jpg)

## 1973-10-06

On October 6, 1973, on the Jewish holy day of Yom Kippur, a coalition of Arab states led by Egypt and Syria launched a surprise attack against Israel. The coordinated offensive aimed to regain territories lost to Israel in the 1967 Six-Day War, specifically the Sinai Peninsula and the Golan Heights. Initial Arab advances caught Israeli forces off guard, leading to significant early gains. The conflict escalated into a major proxy confrontation between the United States and the Soviet Union. It profoundly impacted Middle Eastern politics and global oil prices.
