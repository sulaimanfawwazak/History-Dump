## 1998-09-04

On September 4, 1998, Larry Page and Sergey Brin officially incorporated Google Inc. in Menlo Park, California. The two Stanford University Ph.D. students had developed a new search engine algorithm called "Backrub," which ranked web pages based on the number of links pointing to them, rather than just keyword density. This innovative approach quickly made Google a dominant force in the nascent internet industry. Their garage office served as the initial headquarters for what would become one of the world's most influential technology companies. This event profoundly shaped information access and digital advertising for decades to come.
