# Chilean Military Coup Overthrows Salvador Allende

![Chilean Military Coup Overthrows Salvador Allende](https://www.zinnedproject.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/Salvador-Allende-President.jpg)

## 1973-09-11

On September 11, 1973, a military coup d'état, led by General Augusto Pinochet, overthrew the democratically elected government of President Salvador Allende in Chile. The coup began in the early morning with the Chilean Navy and Air Force attacking key installations, including the presidential palace, La Moneda, in Santiago. President Allende, a socialist, refused to resign and reportedly died by suicide during the assault on the palace. This event marked the end of a long period of democratic rule in Chile and ushered in a brutal 17-year military dictatorship under Pinochet, characterized by severe human rights abuses and political repression.
