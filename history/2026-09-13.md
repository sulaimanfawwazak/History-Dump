## 1993-09-13

On September 13, 1993, a historic agreement known as the Oslo Accords was officially signed on the South Lawn of the White House in Washington D.C. This landmark declaration of principles was signed by Israeli Prime Minister Yitzhak Rabin and PLO Chairman Yasser Arafat, in the presence of U.S. President Bill Clinton. The accord aimed to create a framework for future relations between Israelis and Palestinians, setting out a plan for Palestinian self-rule in the West Bank and Gaza Strip. It represented a significant step towards resolving the Israeli-Palestinian conflict, leading to the establishment of the Palestinian Authority. Although hailed as a breakthrough, the implementation of the accords proved challenging and controversial in subsequent years.
