## 1905-10-26

On October 26, 1905, Norway formally dissolved its union with Sweden, gaining full independence after centuries of foreign rule. This separation followed a period of growing Norwegian nationalism and a constitutional crisis earlier in the year regarding the king's authority over Norwegian foreign policy. King Oscar II of Sweden renounced his claim to the Norwegian throne on this date, marking the peaceful end of the union. The event led to the election of Prince Carl of Denmark as King Haakon VII of Norway, establishing a new monarchy. This peaceful resolution set an important precedent for self-determination in Europe.
