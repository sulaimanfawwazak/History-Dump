# Discovery of Penicillin

![Discovery of Penicillin](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/ace/standard/976/cpsprodpb/59C2/production/_103287922_gettyimages-2667900.jpg)

## 1928-09-28

Scottish bacteriologist Alexander Fleming made a groundbreaking discovery on this day at St. Mary's Hospital in London. While studying Staphylococcus bacteria, he noticed that a blue-green mold, Penicillium notatum, had contaminated one of his petri dishes and was preventing the bacteria from growing around it. Recognizing the potential significance, Fleming isolated the mold and identified its antibacterial properties, dubbing the active substance "penicillin." This accidental observation marked the beginning of the antibiotic era, fundamentally revolutionizing medicine and eventually saving countless lives worldwide by providing effective treatment for bacterial infections.
