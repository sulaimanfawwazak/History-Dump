## 1869-10-02

Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi, who would later become known as Mahatma Gandhi, was born in Porbandar, Gujarat, British India. A preeminent leader of the Indian independence movement, he championed nonviolent civil disobedience, which profoundly influenced movements for civil rights and freedom across the world. His philosophy of Satyagraha, or truth-force, advocated for resistance to tyranny through mass nonviolent resistance. Gandhi's birth anniversary is celebrated worldwide as the International Day of Non-Violence.
