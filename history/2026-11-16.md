# Oklahoma Becomes 46th U.S. State

![Oklahoma Becomes 46th U.S. State](https://humanities.utulsa.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/23/2018/08/Sequoyah_map-933x1024.jpg)

## 1907-11-16

On November 16, 1907, Oklahoma officially joined the United States as its 46th state. The proclamation was signed by President Theodore Roosevelt, uniting the former Oklahoma Territory and Indian Territory into a single entity. This event marked the culmination of significant westward expansion and the controversial displacement of numerous Native American tribes from their ancestral lands. The new state's capital was initially established in Guthrie, before later being moved to Oklahoma City. Its admission represented a significant milestone in American territorial development and national expansion.
