# First Manned Hot-Air Balloon Flight

![First Manned Hot-Air Balloon Flight](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/lookaside/crawler/media/?media_id=10156710218529030)

## 1783-11-21

On November 21, 1783, Jean-François Pilâtre de Rozier and François Laurent d'Arlandes made history by completing the first untethered manned flight in a hot-air balloon. Taking off from the Château de la Muette in Paris, France, their Montgolfière balloon ascended to an estimated altitude of 500 feet. The historic flight covered approximately 5.5 miles in about 25 minutes, landing safely in Butte-aux-Cailles. This pioneering achievement, witnessed by a large crowd including Benjamin Franklin, marked a pivotal moment in human aviation and ignited widespread fascination with aerial travel.
