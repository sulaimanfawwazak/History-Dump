## 1957-09-25

On September 25, 1957, President Dwight D. Eisenhower ordered federal troops from the 101st Airborne Division to Little Rock, Arkansas. This unprecedented action was taken to enforce the desegregation of Little Rock Central High School, following a direct defiance of a federal court order by Arkansas Governor Orval Faubus. The "Little Rock Nine," a group of African American students, had been repeatedly blocked from entering the school by the Arkansas National Guard and angry white mobs. Eisenhower's intervention marked a pivotal moment in the American Civil Rights Movement, affirming the federal government's commitment to upholding the Supreme Court's ruling in Brown v. Board of Education.
