# Balfour Declaration Issued

![Balfour Declaration Issued](https://i0.wp.com/jcfa.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/02/1743866_original.jpg?fit=768%2C1011&ssl=1)

## 1917-11-02

The Balfour Declaration was a public statement issued by the British government during World War I, expressing its support for the establishment of a "national home for the Jewish people" in Palestine. Contained in a letter from Foreign Secretary Arthur Balfour to Lord Rothschild, a leader of the British Jewish community, it aimed to garner Jewish support for the Allied cause. The declaration stipulated that nothing should be done to prejudice the civil and religious rights of existing non-Jewish communities in Palestine. Despite this caveat, it became a foundational document for the Zionist movement, offering international legitimacy to their aspirations. This pivotal declaration profoundly influenced the post-war partitioning of the Ottoman Empire and laid significant groundwork for the future Israeli-Palestinian conflict.
