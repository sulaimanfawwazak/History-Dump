# USS Indianapolis Torpedoed

![USS Indianapolis Torpedoed](https://cdn.britannica.com/68/183268-050-C3485907/World-War-II-USS-Indianapolis-submarine-timeline-casualties-July-30-1945.jpg)

## 1945-07-30

The heavy cruiser USS Indianapolis (CA-35) was torpedoed and sunk by the Imperial Japanese Navy submarine I-58 in the Philippine Sea shortly after midnight on July 30, 1945. The ship had just completed a top-secret mission delivering components for the atomic bomb "Little Boy" to Tinian. Of the 1,195 crewmen aboard, approximately 300 went down with the ship. The remaining 900 faced days in the open ocean, battling exposure, dehydration, and shark attacks, due to a delay in discovery. Only 316 survivors were rescued after four and a half days, making it the greatest single loss of life at sea from a single ship in the history of the U.S. Navy.
