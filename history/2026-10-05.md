# Women's March on Versailles

![Women's March on Versailles](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/57/A_Versailles%2C_%C3%A0_Versailles_5_octobre_1789_-_Restoration.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=index&utm_content=original)

## 1789-10-05

On October 5, 1789, thousands of Parisian women, primarily fishwives and market women, initiated a march from Paris to the Palace of Versailles. Their discontent stemmed from the severe bread shortages and soaring prices, which they attributed to the perceived extravagance and indifference of the royal court. Joined by revolutionary sympathizers and elements of the National Guard, the massive crowd ultimately stormed the palace, demanding bread and the king's return to Paris. The following day, King Louis XVI, Marie Antoinette, and their family were compelled to leave Versailles and relocate to the Tuileries Palace in Paris. This pivotal event significantly diminished the monarchy's independence and brought the royal family under the direct influence of the Parisian populace, accelerating the course of the French Revolution.
