# Treaty of Paris Signed, Ending American Revolutionary War

![Treaty of Paris Signed, Ending American Revolutionary War](https://www.masshist.org/imhs/cms/assets/cms1/treaty_pg1.jpg)

## 1783-09-03

The Treaty of Paris was officially signed on September 3, 1783, in Paris, France, by representatives of King George III of Great Britain and representatives of the United States of America. This momentous treaty formally ended the American Revolutionary War, recognizing the United States as an independent and sovereign nation. Key negotiators included John Adams, Benjamin Franklin, and John Jay for the U.S., and David Hartley for Great Britain. The agreement established the boundaries for the new nation, ranging from the Atlantic Ocean to the Mississippi River, and from Canada to Florida, marking a pivotal moment in American history.
