# Hungarian Revolution of 1956 Begins

![Hungarian Revolution of 1956 Begins](https://worldtreasures.org/assets/uploads/documents/hungarian_revolution.jpg)

## 1956-10-23

On October 23, 1956, a student demonstration in Budapest, Hungary, escalated into a nationwide revolt against the Soviet-imposed government. Thousands of Hungarians joined the protest, demanding political freedoms, the withdrawal of Soviet troops, and an end to communist rule. The uprising quickly spread, leading to violent clashes with state security forces and the toppling of statues of Stalin. While initially successful in forcing some concessions, the revolution was brutally suppressed by a full-scale Soviet invasion in early November, resulting in thousands of deaths and widespread repression.
