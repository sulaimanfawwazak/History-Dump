## 1990-10-03

On October 3, 1990, the German Democratic Republic (East Germany) formally dissolved and its states acceded to the Federal Republic of Germany (West Germany). This historic event marked the end of over four decades of division that began after World War II, bringing together the two German states into a single sovereign nation. The reunification was the culmination of the Peaceful Revolution of 1989, symbolized by the fall of the Berlin Wall, and was achieved through the Two Plus Four Agreement. It had profound implications for European politics and the global balance of power, solidifying Germany's role as a major player on the international stage.
