## 1066-10-14

On October 14, 1066, the Battle of Hastings was fought between the Norman-French army of William, Duke of Normandy, and an English army led by King Harold Godwinson. The battle took place at Senlac Hill, approximately seven miles northwest of Hastings, England. After a day of intense fighting, King Harold was killed, marking a decisive victory for the Normans. This pivotal event led to the Norman Conquest of England, profoundly changing the course of English history, language, and culture. William was subsequently crowned King of England on Christmas Day, 1066, establishing the Norman dynasty.
