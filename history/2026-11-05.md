## 1605-11-05

On November 5, 1605, a plot by a group of English Catholic conspirators, including Guy Fawkes, to assassinate King James I and blow up the House of Lords during the State Opening of Parliament was foiled. The conspirators, led by Robert Catesby, sought to restore a Catholic monarch to the English throne. Fawkes was discovered in the cellars beneath the Palace of Westminster guarding barrels of gunpowder. His capture and the subsequent failure of the plot led to the establishment of Bonfire Night, an annual commemoration in the United Kingdom.
