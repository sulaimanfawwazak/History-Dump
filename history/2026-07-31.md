# Apollo 15 Astronauts Drive On The Moon

![Apollo 15 Astronauts Drive On The Moon](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/lookaside/crawler/media/?media_id=122193750032384091)

## 1971-07-31

On July 31, 1971, Apollo 15 astronauts David Scott and James Irwin made history by becoming the first humans to drive a vehicle on the surface of the Moon. Using the Lunar Roving Vehicle (LRV), nicknamed 'rover,' they traveled farther from their landing site than any previous mission, exploring the Hadley Rille-Apennine region. The LRV significantly expanded the range and scientific capabilities of lunar exploration during their three days on the lunar surface. This mission marked a major advancement in the Apollo program's capacity for in-situ geological investigation and sample collection, greatly enhancing our understanding of lunar geology.
