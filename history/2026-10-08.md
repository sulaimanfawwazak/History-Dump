## 1956-10-08

On October 8, 1956, New York Yankees pitcher Don Larsen accomplished a singular feat by throwing the only perfect game in World Series history. Facing the Brooklyn Dodgers in Game 5 at Yankee Stadium, Larsen retired all 27 batters in order, allowing no hits, walks, or errors. His unprecedented performance led the Yankees to a crucial 2-0 victory. This monumental achievement, a highlight of baseball lore, remains an unparalleled moment in the sport. The Yankees ultimately won the 1956 World Series in seven games.
