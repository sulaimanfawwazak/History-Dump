# People's Republic of China Proclaimed

![People's Republic of China Proclaimed](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/85/Mao_Proclaiming_New_China.JPG?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=index&utm_content=original)

## 1949-10-01

On October 1, 1949, Mao Zedong, Chairman of the Communist Party of China, officially proclaimed the establishment of the People's Republic of China from Tiananmen Square in Beijing. This momentous declaration followed the Communist Party's decisive victory in the Chinese Civil War against the Nationalist government of the Kuomintang. The event marked a monumental shift in global geopolitics, establishing a new communist state and concluding decades of war and political upheaval in China. Mao famously declared that "the Chinese people have stood up," signifying the end of foreign domination and the beginning of a new era for the nation. The defeated Nationalist government, led by Chiang Kai-shek, subsequently retreated to Taiwan.
