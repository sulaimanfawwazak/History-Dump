## 1912-11-28

On November 28, 1912, Albania declared its independence from the Ottoman Empire in the city of Vlorë. This pivotal moment followed centuries of Ottoman rule and occurred amidst the escalating First Balkan War, as the Ottoman Empire was rapidly losing control over its European territories. Ismail Qemali, a prominent Albanian statesman, played a crucial role, raising the Albanian flag and proclaiming the nation's sovereignty. An assembly of Albanian notables and representatives convened to establish a provisional government for the newly independent state. This declaration marked the birth of modern Albania, though its borders and international recognition would be debated and finalized in subsequent years.
