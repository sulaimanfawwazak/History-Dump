# Oregon Attempts To Dispose Of Whale Carcass With Explosives

![Oregon Attempts To Dispose Of Whale Carcass With Explosives](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/0/05/Exploding_whale_%28screengrab%29.jpg/250px-Exploding_whale_%28screengrab%29.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)

## 1970-11-12

On November 12, 1970, the Oregon Department of Transportation (ODOT) attempted a highly unusual method to remove a 45-foot (14 m) sperm whale carcass that had washed ashore near Florence, Oregon. Facing the challenge of disposing of the rotting remains, officials decided to use half a ton of dynamite to explode the whale into small, manageable pieces. However, the plan backfired spectacularly as large chunks of blubber rained down on beachgoers, spectators, and nearby parked cars, covering the area in putrid debris. While no serious injuries were reported, at least one car was damaged, and the cleanup effort was extensive. The event garnered significant media attention and has since become a famous example of a public works project gone wrong.
