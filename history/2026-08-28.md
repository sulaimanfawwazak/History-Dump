## 1963-08-28

On August 28, 1963, civil rights leader Martin Luther King Jr. delivered his iconic 'I Have a Dream' speech from the steps of the Lincoln Memorial in Washington, D.C. This powerful address was the climax of the March on Washington for Jobs and Freedom, which gathered an estimated 250,000 participants advocating for racial equality and justice. King's eloquent words articulated his vision of a future where all Americans would be judged by the content of their character, not by the color of their skin. The speech became a defining moment of the American Civil Rights Movement, profoundly influencing public opinion and significantly contributing to the passage of the Civil Rights Act of 1964.
