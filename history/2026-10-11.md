# Apollo 7 Launches, First Crewed Mission

![Apollo 7 Launches, First Crewed Mission](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/s68-33744.jpeg)

## 1968-10-11

On October 11, 1968, Apollo 7, the first crewed mission in NASA's Apollo program, successfully launched from Cape Kennedy, Florida. Aboard the Apollo Command/Service Module were astronauts Wally Schirra, Donn Eisele, and Walt Cunningham. The mission's primary objective was to thoroughly test the spacecraft's systems in Earth orbit, particularly after the tragic Apollo 1 fire. Over 11 days, the crew successfully performed numerous maneuvers and conducted the first live television broadcast from an American spacecraft. This crucial mission validated the command module's design and operational readiness, paving the way for future lunar missions, including the historic Apollo 11 moon landing.
