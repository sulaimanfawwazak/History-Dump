## 1963-11-23

On November 23, 1963, the iconic British science fiction television series "Doctor Who" broadcast its very first episode, titled "An Unearthly Child," on BBC Television. The show introduced viewers to the mysterious time-travelling alien known as the Doctor, who journeys through space and time in his TARDIS, a police box that is "bigger on the inside." Starring William Hartnell as the First Doctor, the program quickly became a cultural phenomenon, captivating audiences with its imaginative stories and educational elements. Its debut marked the beginning of one of the longest-running and most beloved science fiction franchises in television history, influencing generations of viewers and creators.
