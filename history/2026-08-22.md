# Battle of Bosworth Field

## 1485-08-22

On August 22, 1485, the Battle of Bosworth Field was fought near Market Bosworth, Leicestershire, England. This decisive engagement marked the end of the Wars of the Roses, the civil conflict between the Houses of Lancaster and York. King Richard III, the last Plantagenet king, was killed during the battle, famously losing his horse and reportedly fighting to the death. His defeat paved the way for Henry Tudor, Earl of Richmond, to ascend to the throne as King Henry VII, establishing the Tudor dynasty. The battle thus heralded a new era in English history and the eventual unification of the warring factions.
