## 1945-08-14

On August 14, 1945, Imperial Japan officially accepted the terms of the Potsdam Declaration, agreeing to an unconditional surrender to the Allied powers. This pivotal decision, announced by Emperor Hirohito the following day, effectively brought an end to World War II. The acceptance came shortly after the devastating atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki, and the Soviet Union's declaration of war and invasion of Japanese-held Manchuria. While the formal surrender ceremonies would occur on September 2 aboard the USS Missouri, August 14th was widely celebrated by Allied nations as "Victory over Japan Day" (V-J Day). This moment marked the cessation of the deadliest conflict in human history, leading to widespread jubilation across the globe.
