# Assassination of President John F. Kennedy

![Assassination of President John F. Kennedy](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/59/JFK_Motorcade_GettyImages-517330536.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=index&utm_content=original)

## 1963-11-22

On November 22, 1963, John F. Kennedy, the 35th President of the United States, was tragically assassinated in Dallas, Texas. He was riding in a motorcade with his wife, Jacqueline Kennedy, and Governor John Connally of Texas, when he was shot by a sniper. Lee Harvey Oswald, a former Marine, was arrested shortly after the shooting and charged with the murder. The assassination sent shockwaves across the nation and the world, leading to a profound period of grief and national mourning. Vice President Lyndon B. Johnson was sworn in as President aboard Air Force One just hours later.
