## 1945-11-20

On November 20, 1945, the International Military Tribunal began its proceedings against 24 prominent Nazi leaders in Nuremberg, Germany. This landmark series of military tribunals prosecuted individuals responsible for war crimes, crimes against peace, and crimes against humanity committed during World War II. Organized by the Allied powers—the United States, Great Britain, France, and the Soviet Union—the trials aimed to hold high-ranking officials accountable for their actions and establish a new precedent in international law. The trials lasted nearly a year, concluding with convictions for many defendants and setting a foundation for future international criminal justice.
