# Nellie Bly Begins Record-Breaking Round-the-World Journey

## 1889-11-14

On November 14, 1889, American journalist Elizabeth Cochrane Seaman, widely known by her pen name Nellie Bly, commenced an ambitious journey to circumnavigate the globe. Inspired by Jules Verne's novel *Around the World in Eighty Days*, Bly aimed to beat the fictional record of 80 days. She departed from Hoboken, New Jersey, traveling alone with minimal luggage, using steamships and trains as her primary modes of transportation. This daring expedition was commissioned by the *New York World* newspaper, which provided extensive coverage of her progress. Bly successfully completed her global trek in a remarkable 72 days, 6 hours, 11 minutes, and 14 seconds, securing her place as a trailblazing figure in journalism and adventure.
