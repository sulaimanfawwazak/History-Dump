# Voyager 2 Spacecraft Launched

![Voyager 2 Spacecraft Launched](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/voyager_2_4_launch.jpg)

## 1977-08-20

On August 20, 1977, NASA launched the Voyager 2 robotic probe from Cape Canaveral, Florida, marking the beginning of an unprecedented exploration of the outer solar system. Voyager 2 was sent on a trajectory that would eventually take it to Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune, becoming the only spacecraft to ever visit these two ice giants. It provided the first close-up images and scientific data from Uranus and Neptune, revealing their unique atmospheric features and moon systems. Still operational today, Voyager 2 continues to send back data from interstellar space, contributing immensely to humanity's understanding of our solar system and beyond.
