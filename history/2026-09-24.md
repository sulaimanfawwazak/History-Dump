# President Eisenhower Deploys Troops to Little Rock

![President Eisenhower Deploys Troops to Little Rock](https://static01.nyt.com/images/section/learning/general/onthisday/big/0925_big.gif)

## 1957-09-24

On September 24, 1957, President Dwight D. Eisenhower issued Executive Order 10730, deploying elements of the 101st Airborne Division to Little Rock, Arkansas. This historic action was taken to enforce the desegregation of Little Rock Central High School, following a federal court order and Arkansas Governor Orval Faubus's defiant obstruction. The troops were tasked with protecting the nine African American students, famously known as the Little Rock Nine, as they attempted to attend the previously all-white school. This intervention marked a crucial moment in the American Civil Rights Movement, affirming the federal government's commitment to upholding the Supreme Court's Brown v. Board of Education ruling.
