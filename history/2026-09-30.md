## 1938-09-30

On September 30, 1938, the Munich Agreement was signed by representatives from Germany, France, the United Kingdom, and Italy. This accord permitted Nazi Germany's annexation of Czechoslovakia's Sudetenland, a region bordering Germany with a significant German-speaking population. British Prime Minister Neville Chamberlain famously declared "peace for our time" upon his return, believing he had averted war. However, the agreement is widely regarded as a failed act of appeasement, as Hitler soon violated its terms by invading the rest of Czechoslovakia the following year, a clear prelude to World War II. It remains a controversial symbol of the dangers of negotiating with aggressive powers.
