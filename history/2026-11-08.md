# Wilhelm Röntgen Discovers X-rays

![Wilhelm Röntgen Discovers X-rays](https://www.perlove.net/wp-content/uploads/Wilhelm-Conrad-Rontgen-and-the-first-x-ray-picture-Wilhelm-Conrad-Rontgen-and-the-first-x-ray-picture.jpg)

## 1895-11-08

On November 8, 1895, German physicist Wilhelm Conrad Röntgen made a groundbreaking discovery at the Physical Institute of the University of Würzburg. While experimenting with cathode rays and a Crookes tube, he observed a fluorescent glow coming from a barium platinocyanide screen placed nearby. He realized this radiation could penetrate various materials and named them "X-rays" due to their unknown nature. This revolutionary discovery quickly transformed medicine, allowing doctors to non-invasively view the internal structures of the human body. Röntgen was awarded the first Nobel Prize in Physics in 1901 for this profound contribution to science.
