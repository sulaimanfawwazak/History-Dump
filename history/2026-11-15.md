## 1889-11-15

On November 15, 1889, Brazil transitioned from a monarchy to a republic through a military coup. Led by Marshal Deodoro da Fonseca, the coup successfully deposed Emperor Dom Pedro II, who had reigned for nearly five decades. The royal family was subsequently exiled, and the United States of Brazil was officially proclaimed. This pivotal event marked the end of the Brazilian Empire and ushered in a new republican era for the South American nation. The change fundamentally reshaped Brazil's political and social structure, laying the groundwork for its modern state.
